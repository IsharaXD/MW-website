Our Journey

Decades of excellence in textile manufacturing.

1974

Foundation at Almoor Street

A small factory was established in Rathnam Building, Almoor Street, Colombo with about 35 machines and workers, where the famous "Maliban" brand shirts were produced.

1979

New Colombo 10 Facility

A new facility was established in Colombo 10, expanding operations with 100 Juki machines and 500 employees across sewing, cutting and finishing departments.

1981

Began Garment Exports

The company began exporting garments through an agency, later setting up its own shipping department under the Maliban name. Its first brand exported was "MAST."

1986

Four-Story Expansion

By 1986, EAM Maliban established its main branch in a four-story building at Sri Dhamma Mawatha, Colombo 10, with state-of-the-art facilities and over 1,200 employees.

1994

EAM Maliban Textile (Pvt) Ltd

In 1994, under the leadership of Chairman Mr. Eliyas Abdul Kareem, the company was restructured and commenced operations as EAM Maliban Textile (Pvt) Ltd.

2024

Renamed to Maliban Wovens

In 2024, the name EAM Maliban Textile (Pvt) Ltd was changed to Maliban Wovens (Pvt) Ltd. In textile excellence and global reach, in every stitch. Future-forward mindset.
